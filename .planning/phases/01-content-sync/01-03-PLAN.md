## Edge coverage and flagged assumptions (spec-less fallback)

Flagged assumption (edge probe SYNC-03 = unclassified, left unresolved): an e-mail whose domain is a subdomain of an allow-listed domain (for example `team.digitiamo.com`) is kept; any other domain is masked. Calendar-group and vendor reply-to addresses are therefore masked by default; Joshua decides in plan 08 whether to add their domains to `REDACT_EMAIL_ALLOW_DOMAINS` (research Open Question 3).
Flagged assumption (research A8, refined): the entropy rule fires on runs of 24+ characters from `[A-Za-z0-9+/=_-]` with at least one letter and one digit and Shannon entropy >= 4.2 bits/char, skipping URLs, UUIDs, already-masked spans and pure-hex strings (Guides pin actions by 40-char commit SHA); hex-only secrets therefore rely on the label and prefix rules.
Flagged assumption (research A10): only image links are reduced to alt text, as D-17 states; non-image ClickUp attachment links keep their URL. Raised with Joshua in plan 08.
