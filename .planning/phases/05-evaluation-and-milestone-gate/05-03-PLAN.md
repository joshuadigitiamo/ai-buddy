## Decision amendment: D-20 collect-then-score (RESEARCH A8, accepted by Joshua's "accept all your recommendations")

D-20 literally says `mlflow.genai.evaluate` with a `predict_fn` wrapping `run_workflow`. RESEARCH executed that design and found that a cached `predict_fn` result produces a hollow trace (EVAL-05 broken on every rerun), that evaluate makes one extra real `predict_fn` call for trace validation, and that it defaults to 10 workers. The amendment keeps D-20's intent (verdicts attach to each judged trace, run-level metrics are logged, every judged answer is inspectable) and changes the mechanism:
- Collect phase: the runner calls `run_workflow` inside `sink.traced("bot_run", ...)` (an `@mlflow.trace(name="bot_run")` root in the 05-04 adapter), logs the item's expectations on the trace, and returns `{outcome, trace_id}` (cached from 05-07 on).
- Score phase: the runner hands precomputed verdicts to `sink.score` (in the 05-04 adapter: `mlflow.genai.evaluate(data=<list of Trace>, scorers=[...])` with custom scorers that only read those verdicts; deterministic checks here, judge verdicts from 05-08). No `predict_fn`.
- The gate reads the local verdicts (and from 05-07 the local cache), never MLflow results or the UI.
This is recorded as an amendment, not a new user decision.
